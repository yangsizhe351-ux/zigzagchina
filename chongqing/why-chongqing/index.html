CHONGQING

The City That Refuses to Be Flat

There is a reason Chongqing feels unlike anywhere else in China.

Perhaps it begins when you realize the street beneath your feet is not the same level as the street above you. Then comes a monorail passing through a residential building, a staircase disappearing into a hillside neighborhood, or two rivers glowing beneath a forest of bridges.

And somewhere along the way, you stop trying to understand Chongqing from a map.

You simply start following it.

01

A City Like Nowhere Else

Where the city goes up, down — and everywhere in between.

A city built in layers.

02

One of China’s Most Cinematic Cities

Come for the skyline. Stay for the atmosphere.

Neon, rivers, bridges and mountains — all layered into one unforgettable city.

03

A City That Comes Alive After Dark

The night is part of the city.

When the sun goes down, Chongqing changes character.

04

A Food Culture With Attitude

Bold. Spicy. Smoky. Communal.

From bubbling hotpot to smoky skewers and late-night noodles, Chongqing food is meant to be shared.

The best meals don’t always come with a view. Sometimes, they come with a table full of locals.

05

A City Shaped by Two Rivers

Where two rivers meet, the city rises.

The Yangtze and Jialing Rivers shape Chongqing’s skyline, neighborhoods and rhythm — creating a city that unfolds differently from every direction.

Two rivers. One extraordinary city.

06

Beyond the Viral Sights

You’ve seen Chongqing online. Now see what lies beyond the frame.

Hongyadong. Liziba. The monorail. The famous 8D city.

But the real Chongqing begins where the viral sights end — in its neighborhoods, food, people and everyday rhythms.

See the city. Then go beyond it.
