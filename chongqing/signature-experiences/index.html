03

A Private Chongqing Hotpot Experience

More than a meal.

Sit down, order boldly, and discover the fiery world of Chongqing hotpot with a local perspective. From choosing the right broth and learning how to order like a local, to dipping, sharing and navigating the perfect balance of spice and flavour, we’ll guide you through the rituals behind one of China’s most iconic meals.

  • Private table
  • Local host
  • Authentic hotpot
  • No tourist set menus

This is Chongqing at its most social, spontaneous, and unapologetically local.

07

Chongqing Beyond the City

There’s more to Chongqing than the skyline.

When you’re ready to venture beyond the city, Chongqing opens into a world of dramatic landscapes, ancient art and places that feel far removed from the energy of downtown.

Wulong

Journey into a landscape shaped by time — towering karst cliffs, deep valleys and the spectacular Three Natural Bridges. A chance to experience a wilder, more cinematic side of Chongqing.

  • Karst Landscapes
  • Three Natural Bridges
  • Scenic Hiking
  • Nature

Dazu

Step into a quieter world of stone, faith and craftsmanship. Discover extraordinary cliff carvings that reveal centuries of Buddhist art and the cultural depth of southwestern China.

  • Buddhist Art
  • Stone Carvings
  • UNESCO World Heritage
  • Cultural Discovery

Beyond the city, Chongqing becomes a journey through landscape, history and time.
