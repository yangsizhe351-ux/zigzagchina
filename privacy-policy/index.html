Privacy Policy — Draft

This draft explains the travel-brief form in this preview. It is not a published privacy policy. Please use fictional details only.

01

Information in the form

The form asks for travel preferences, dates, budget and contact details. First name, last name, email and country/region are required by the current form, along with the contact-confirmation checkbox. Other answers, including WhatsApp, WeChat, children's age groups and special requirements, are optional. Do not enter passport details, payment details, medical information or children's names in this preview.

02

What happens in this preview

Your entries are used to display, edit and review your travel brief. Confirming it demonstrates the thank-you screen: the form does not send your answers and no follow-up will be started. The intended purpose of a future live form is to respond to your travel enquiry and help plan your trip; its processing arrangements still need confirmation.

03

Temporary answers and language preference

Form answers are held in page memory, not saved by this form in the URL, browser history, local storage or session storage. They are not a saved draft; do not rely on them remaining available after reloading or leaving. Browser autofill is controlled separately by your browser. The site saves your language preference on this device.

04

External services

The current design requests fonts from Google Fonts. These requests are separate from sending the form. Hosting logs, other service providers and any international data transfers still need to be checked before publication.

05

Before this policy can take effect

Operator / data controller: to be confirmed.

Privacy contact: to be confirmed.

Live-service retention and deletion: to be confirmed.

The final notice must explain the legal basis, who receives data, any transfers and safeguards, and how to request access, correction or deletion and exercise other applicable rights. These details and the handling of children's and sensitive information must be confirmed before the form accepts real enquiries.
